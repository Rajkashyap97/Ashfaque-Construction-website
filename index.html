Frequently Asked Questions

Find answers to the most common questions our clients ask.

What types of projects do you handle?
We handle residential, commercial, and industrial projects including new builds, renovations, interior design, and project management across East Champaran.
How long does a typical construction project take?
Timelines vary based on project size and complexity. A standard home takes 6-12 months, while renovations may take 2-6 months. We provide a detailed timeline before starting.
Do you provide free estimates/quotations?
Yes, we offer free site visits and quotations. Contact us via phone or WhatsApp, and we'll schedule a consultation at your convenience.
What areas do you serve in Bihar?
We primarily serve Motihari, Bettiah, Chakia, and entire East Champaran district. We also take up projects in nearby districts upon request.
